# The argument made for a different reading

The backing of the currency by creative output is an original feature of the design and is read as backing what the Source pays. At full backing the design's model clears the bar in every seed (91.1% of participants at $60,000 or more), and the simulation's BLEI ladder agrees (76.2% Flourishing). The reading reopens toward 0.5 on an empirical study showing that the backing does not hold in real-world data.
