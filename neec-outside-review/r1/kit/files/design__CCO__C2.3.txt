Standard welfare metrics measure the level of income or wealth at a single point in time. [Research Hub: basic-living-economic-index.html] 9.1 On "Comfortable" vs. "Flourishing": What BLEI Does and Does Not Measure The terminology introduced in Section 4 — "Comfortable" for baseline/CCO-only configurations reaching the 730-day tier, "Flourishing" for configurations where CCO and PTF are jointly active — reflects a deliberate narrowing of what this paper claims BLEI measures. BLEI is a measure of economic temporal stability: the number of days a household's accessible resources can sustain basic living.
