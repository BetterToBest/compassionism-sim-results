What is simulated A fixed population of 500 working-age adults, living in households as US adults do with their children (since v5.3; section 6.13), is followed year by year for 20 years (or 40), twice on the same random draws: once with Compassionism as specified on the Research Hub (a monthly allowance of Basic Units that buy only essentials and expire; expired units converted to dollars at earned rates through project work; community-owned essential-service providers, PTFs; community housing, PTH; zone coordination, SZH; a civic platform, CIP), paid for by a Source that issues the units, and once with no programme. The model tracks each adult's wage, cost of living, savings (net wealth), programme status and conversion income, and the economy-wide price level that the programme's new money and extra demand for essentials move. It reports poverty (three measures for adults, and children's poverty and household measures since v5.3), prices, cost, work and inequality as the average over 500 paired runs (seeds 1 to 500), in three environments. ## 2.
