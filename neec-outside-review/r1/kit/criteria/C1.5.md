# C1.5 Universal Wealth Access

**Requirement.** All participants access wealth-building mechanisms, not merely income transfers. Prevent a permanent wealth-excluded underclass — measured by *breadth of access*, not by the resulting distribution's evenness (that is now C1.2b's question, not this one).

**Pass threshold.** ≥80% population with an active wealth-accumulation pathway

**Measurement.** Percentage of population with an active wealth accumulation pathway (not just eligibility, but an actual mechanism producing asset growth).

**Threshold note.** Gini clause removed, moved to C1.2b, Paper Section 12.2 (commentary moved out of the threshold in v2.0).

**Distinguishes.** Equality of access (required — this criterion) from equality of outcomes (not required, and never was) and from bounded concentration (a separate question, now C1.2b — see Section 12.2 for why these were split apart rather than left combined).

**Rationale.** Systems limiting wealth-building *access* to a subset of participants — whether by capital ownership, employment status, or cooperative membership — leave the excluded group structurally dependent regardless of how equitable the wealth distribution is among those who do have access.

**Derivation.** From N4 (Universal Wealth Access) + N7 (Anti-Capture) — unchanged from legacy C1.5.

**Pass Threshold, clause by clause** (all must be shown cleared for 1.0; protocol 2.3):

1. ≥80% population with an active wealth-accumulation pathway

**Note.** This band's reasoning is unchanged from legacy C1.5's anchors (H.7 above); narrowing the threshold doesn't change which systems clear an access-breadth bar, only removes a second, now-separate test that used to ride alongside it. the band texts stand.

**Anchor bands** (they describe; the Pass Threshold governs, protocol 4.6):

- **1.0** Broad-based access not contingent on prior wealth or narrow membership.
- **0.5** Access exists but is gated such that a meaningful population segment is excluded.
- **0.0** Access concentrated among those already positioned to have it.
