# C4.5 Exploitation Elimination

**Requirement.** Remove extractive relationships, not merely regulate them. Structural transformation, not incremental reform.

**Pass threshold.** Extraction rates <10% GDP, genuine exit rights from exploitative relationships

**Measurement.** Extraction rates (wealth transfer from labor to capital, tenants to landlords, borrowers to lenders). Power asymmetries (capacity to refuse participation without penalty). The share of decisions made under duress is measured once, in C2.1.

**Rationale.** Systems maintaining landlord-tenant dynamics ($380,000 extraction over 20 years), employer-employee asymmetries (surplus value extraction), or creditor-debtor subordination (wealth transfer through interest) cannot claim to eliminate exploitation—they merely adjust terms.

**Derivation.** From N3 (Coercion Minimization) + N4 (Universal Wealth Access)

**Pass Threshold, clause by clause** (all must be shown cleared for 1.0; protocol 2.3):

1. Extraction rates <10% GDP
2. genuine exit rights from exploitative relationships

**Note.** Examples are re-estimated in the rescoring pass.

**Anchor bands** (they describe; the Pass Threshold governs, protocol 4.6):

- **1.0** Structural elimination of extractive relationships (landlord-tenant, employer-employee, creditor-debtor), not merely regulation of their terms.
- **0.5** Reduced but not eliminated extraction, or elimination in one relationship type (e.g., capital-labor) while another persists (e.g., inter-firm competition).
- **0.0** Extraction is a structural, load-bearing feature of the system rather than an incidental flaw.
