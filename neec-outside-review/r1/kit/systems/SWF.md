# Sovereign Wealth Fund Statism

**Kind:** mechanism. An institution, instrument or policy family that operates inside a wider economy without itself specifying that economy. It is scored as though it governed every part of an economy it could govern; a country's outcomes are credited to it only where the mechanism does the work.

**Configuration:** Sovereign wealth funds that return resource or investment income to the public, in two variants read together: general budget support (Norway's Government Pension Fund Global) and a direct dividend (Alaska's Permanent Fund and its dividend). Gulf-state and Singaporean funds are outside this entry.
