### Reservation wages under the Nash relation (wage = (1 - pi) x what the worker adds + pi x reservation wage)

Before the programme the relation puts the reservation wage at (1 - (1 - pi)/m)/pi of the wage; the programme adds the outside-option rise (P10, median, P90 of the year-20 workers, as a share of their full-year earnings). A negative starting value means the Nash relation cannot give that pass-through with that markdown (the puzzle Jager et al. report).
