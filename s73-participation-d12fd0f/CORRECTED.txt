Partly corrected (Nov 18, 2026). The ageing rows with the labour-force flows (agP and agS) in this pilot measured a defect.

With ageing, a new adult who started outside the labour force kept the wage it entered with but not the price level of the time,
so when it later went back to work the engine raised the wage by all the price rises since year 0, counting prices twice. Such
wages then fed the average that set later new adults' wages. It needs ageing, the yearly flows and moving prices, so in this
pilot it reached agP and agS: their programme runs in all three environments, and their no-programme runs in Adverse and Stress
(outside inflation). Nothing else in this folder is reached: agL0 and every row without ageing reproduce exactly on the fixed
engine.

Do not quote agP or agS figures from this folder, nor the ageing contrasts (agP - agL0, agS - agL0). Their corrected values, with
every other row unchanged, are in s73-ageing-b362152/ (tables.md there).
